The AI Dilemma

AI adoption for SMEs is full of interest and full of noise — rarely full of clarity.

AI is accessible — but unclear

Tools are cheaper and easier to access than ever. What's missing isn't access — it's a clear sense of where to start and what actually matters for your business.

75%+ of SMEs experiment with AI but fail to gain value

Most SMEs have already tried something — a chatbot, a pilot, an automation script. Few have turned that experimentation into measurable business value.

The pressure: competitors, vendors, regulators

Competitors are moving, vendors are pitching, and regulators — especially under the EU AI Act — are raising the bar. Standing still carries its own risk.

The paradox: interest without clarity

Interest in AI has never been higher, yet clarity on how to adopt it safely and effectively remains rare. That gap is where most SMEs get stuck.

Personal Productivity

AI tools that amplify individual output — drafting, summarizing, researching, and organizing work faster than manual effort allows.

SME Value

Immediate, low-risk productivity gains without changing core systems or processes.

Examples

  • AI writing assistants
  • Meeting summarizers
  • Email drafting tools
  • Research copilots

Readiness Requirements

Basic digital literacy and willingness to adopt new daily tools; minimal IT involvement.

Where It Fits in the Roadmap

Often the first, lowest-risk step in an AI roadmap — building familiarity before tackling automation or custom builds.

Intelligent Workflow Automations

AI embedded directly into existing business processes to remove repetitive manual steps.

SME Value

Frees staff time from repetitive tasks and reduces error rates in operational workflows.

Examples

  • Automated invoice processing
  • Lead routing
  • Scheduling
  • Document classification

Readiness Requirements

Documented processes and a willingness to standardize workflows before automating them.

Where It Fits in the Roadmap

RAG Solutions

AI becomes a knowledge assistant that operationalizes the organization's collective intelligence.

SME Value

Makes internal knowledge instantly searchable and usable, reducing time lost to finding information.

Examples

  • Internal knowledge assistants
  • Customer support copilots grounded in company documentation
  • Policy Q&A tools

Readiness Requirements

Organized, accessible internal documentation and a clear data governance approach.

Where It Fits in the Roadmap

Mid-roadmap — once workflows are automated, RAG solutions unlock the organization's existing knowledge base.

Assess your readiness →

Reasoning Agents

AI systems that plan and execute multi-step tasks with a degree of autonomy, within defined guardrails.

SME Value

Handles complex, multi-step work that goes beyond single-prompt automation, with human oversight where needed.

Examples

  • Multi-step research agents
  • Autonomous scheduling and coordination agents
  • Agentic customer workflows

Readiness Requirements

Mature governance, clear escalation rules, and monitoring in place before granting autonomy.

Where It Fits in the Roadmap

A later-stage roadmap step, layered on top of workflow automation and RAG once trust and governance are established.

SME Value

Examples

  • Proprietary forecasting models
  • Models fine-tuned on specialized industry data

Readiness Requirements

Sufficient proprietary data, technical capacity (in-house or via The BrokerAI's network), and a clear ROI case.

Our Methodology

The Driving-Triad: a strategic framework built on three vertices — Need, Opportunity, and Readiness.

The roadmap adapts dynamically as these three vertices evolve.

Need

What does the business actually require? We start by identifying the real operational or strategic need — not the flashiest technology.

Opportunity

Where can AI create measurable value? We map opportunities against effort, cost, and impact.

Readiness

Is the organization equipped to execute? We assess skills, data, culture, and governance before committing to a path.
